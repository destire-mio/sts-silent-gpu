### One dev-60 lookahead comparison in evaluation windows

After the ordinary 60h argmax examination, compare the same retained dev-60.pt on the first 256 fixed dev seeds (2000000000–2000000255), eight × 32 games: a timed argmax baseline, turn previews, and combat previews if estimated to fit one hour. The extra 256-game baseline supplies an actual comparable wall time, rather than assigning a fraction of the full 1024-game timing to it.

The existing lookahead policy is unchanged: top four legal candidates × eight shared hidden seeds, the same NoSL mask, confidence at least 0.9 / one legal action / Runic Dome skips, paired mean improvement greater than SE, copied RewardLedger increments plus nonterminal leaf value, terminal death/final victory leaf value zero; turn/combat preview caps remain 64/256. New counters measure all mainline decisions, actual previewed decisions, overrides, and preview steps. The report includes mean floor, Act1/Act2/first Act3 bosses, Act3 wins, Heart wins, paired floor difference and SE against the same 256-game baseline, total wall seconds, preview fraction and override fraction (both per all mainline decisions, with conditional override fraction also retained).

Combat is skipped if four times the measured turn wall time (the 256/64 depth-cap ratio) exceeds 3600 seconds. This is an explicit conservative estimate, not a GPU speed claim; an attempted combat run also has a 3600-second wall cap. Completed game rows and counters persist per rank so allocation-boundary continuation does not repeat completed seeds. Interrupted unfinished games may restart; their spent time remains in the measured total. No result is called complete until all 256 seeds are present.

Execution occurs inside an existing natural allocation, before training resumes or immediately after the completed normal argmax evaluation. The current already imported worker is not patched or paused: the next natural worker carries this hook. If that handoff occurs after dev-60 already exists, the dev-60 supplement runs in that worker's initial evaluation window. The normal argmax completion is retained even if the supplement reaches the allocation deadline, avoiding a repeated ordinary examination. Existing stop-requested fairness receipts and allocation deadlines are honored; no new scheduler query, stop condition, reservation or GPU job is introduced.

Training model/optimizer/scaler/collector/pools/rewards and learner code remain unchanged; the evaluation model is separate and global CPU/CUDA RNG is restored with fork_rng. This preparation has three focused CPU checks only. No GPU lookahead experiment or speed result has yet been produced. The final paired table will be published after actual execution; 512-model training and lookahead during training remain unapproved.
